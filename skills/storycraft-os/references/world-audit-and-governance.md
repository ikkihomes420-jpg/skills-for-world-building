# Canon Governance, World Audits, and Releases

Read this reference when reconciling lore, auditing a world, merging documents, repairing contradictions, preparing a setting release, or maintaining a long-running campaign/world bible. Do not silently regularize established canon.

## 1. Separate States of Knowledge

Every statement belongs to one of these states. Label it at the point of creation.

| State | Meaning | Allowed action |
|---|---|---|
| Approved canon | Author-confirmed source of truth. | Extend compatibly; propose revision with impact analysis. |
| Provisional | Working choice, not yet locked. | Use cautiously and surface for confirmation. |
| Disputed | Sources or in-world accounts conflict. | Preserve sources and model uncertainty. |
| Open decision | A needed choice is not yet made. | Present bounded options and consequences. |
| Deprecated | Superseded but historically relevant record. | Retain provenance; do not generate as current fact. |
| Contradiction | Approved or asserted claims cannot both hold. | Audit, propose repair, await a decision. |

Do not confuse in-world ambiguity with authorial uncertainty. A disputed rumor can be approved canon as a rumor even if its truth value remains unknown.

## 2. Audit in Dependency Order

Start with the issue that blocks simulation or interpretation. Move from high-leverage constraints to surface details.

1. **Reality rules:** physics, metaphysics, technology/magic limits, time/space behavior.
2. **World systems:** ecological, material, economic, political, cultural, and information interfaces.
3. **Entity dependencies:** faction resources, places, institutions, relationships, capabilities.
4. **Timeline and causality:** event order, cause/effect, travel/communication, historical branches.
5. **Knowledge state:** public narrative, actor belief, secrecy, source reliability, reader/player scope.
6. **Active state:** consequences, open threads, resources, actor location/status, session boundary.
7. **Terminology and presentation:** names, aliases, definitions, maps, style, and format.

| Finding type | Evidence needed | Repair approach |
|---|---|---|
| Contradiction | Two incompatible canon records or a rule violated by a record. | Preserve both, identify dependency impact, offer options. |
| Unsupported link | A claimed consequence lacks a causal bridge. | Add bridge, reclassify as possibility, or remove claim. |
| Underspecification | Future generation/simulation cannot decide consistently. | Define a scoped rule or an explicit decision boundary. |
| Scale mismatch | Detail is disconnected from intended use or workload. | Compress, archive, or connect it to an active system. |
| Drift | Recent outputs ignore constraints or repeat a pattern. | Re-ground in state and vary through existing pressures. |
| Deliberate exception | A form violates a general rule intentionally. | Record rationale, scope, and regression test. |

## 3. Generate Repair Options

For material contradictions, provide at least two viable repairs where practical. Typical patterns are: preserve both records through a timing/source distinction; narrow an overly broad rule; revise a later record and update dependents; classify a source as myth/propaganda/dispute; or make a deliberate exception. State impacts on existing entities, maps, timelines, sessions, and external materials.

Use a decision record for any repair that changes a rule, major entity, timeline event, or public release. Record rationale, alternatives rejected, affected IDs, tests, and the author’s approval status.

## 4. Run a World Health Review

At project checkpoints, inspect for four balances rather than a fixed checklist:

- **Causal balance:** Do declared conditions reliably constrain outcomes without making them fully deterministic?
- **Perspective balance:** Does the world have more than one social, cultural, institutional, or geographic vantage where needed?
- **Information balance:** Are secrecy, rumor, public narrative, and actor knowledge traceable rather than arbitrary?
- **Variation balance:** Have cost, conflict, calm, beauty, humor, triumph, and failure been calibrated to the world’s promise rather than flattened into a monotone?

A positive audit does not mean “realistic.” It means the world behaves consistently with its stated reality model and purpose.

## 5. Release and Handoff

Freeze a release before major restructuring or sharing. Include the world manifest/version, changed records, effective decisions, active state snapshot, audit result, unresolved decisions, and compatibility notes. A release can be incomplete if its limitations are visible.

## 6. Improve Storycraft OS Through Use

Convert a real failure into a narrow upgrade only after recording the project context and evidence. Decide whether it is a trigger, artifact schema, routing, validator, or workflow issue; make one targeted change; test it on a representative world record; and retain it only if it improves continuity, causal traceability, or author control. Do not expand the OS merely because a setting could contain more lore.
